Why KareGivv

Finding good home care shouldn't be this hard.

When a family member needs care, most Kenyan families rely on word of mouth, an untrained house help, or one relative carrying it all. KareGivv gives you a better way: a trusted network of professional caregivers, matched to your needs, with secure payment and real accountability.

What we cover

Care across six areas

Whether it's a few hours of companionship or full daily support, we match you with a caregiver trained for the need.

Elderly care

Day-to-day support, mobility help, and companionship for ageing parents and grandparents.

Personal care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting and medication reminders, handled with dignity.

Post-operative recovery

At-home support after surgery: wound-care assistance, mobility, and daily routines.

Palliative & end-of-life

Compassionate comfort care that supports both the patient and the whole family.

Dementia & Alzheimer's

Patient, specialised support for memory loss, routine, and safety at home.

Chronic & special needs

Ongoing support for chronic conditions and disability, tailored to each person.

Plus companion care, respite care and medication management, with medical escort and equipment rentals coming soon.

How it works

1

Tell us who needs care
